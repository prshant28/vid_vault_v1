🎯 Study Strategy Tips
🧠
Active Recall
After each study block, close your notes and write everything you remember. This beats re-reading by 3×.
⏱️
Pomodoro Inside Blocks
Split each study block into 25-min focus + 5-min micro-rest cycles for maximum retention.
💧
Hydrate Every Break
Drink a full glass of water every break. Dehydration cuts cognitive performance by up to 15%.
📵
Phone in Another Room
Physical distance from your phone increases deep focus. Silence isn't enough — out of sight matters.
✍️
Write by Hand
Handwriting notes activates deeper processing than typing. Use your talk breaks to review written notes.
🌙
Protect Sleep
The 3AM plan only works if you sleep by 10–11PM. Quality sleep consolidates everything you studied.
